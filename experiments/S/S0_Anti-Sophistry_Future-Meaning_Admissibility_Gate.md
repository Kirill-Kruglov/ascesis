## 6. Upgrade rules

A claim may upgrade only through explicit gates.

### From FORMED to POETIC

Allowed if:

```text
derivation trace exists
but no consequence obligation exists
```

### From POETIC to SUSPENDED

Allowed if:

```text
claim conflicts with current ontology
but can state what kind of ontology extension would make it meaningful
```

### From SUSPENDED to LOCAL

Allowed if:

```text
scope is explicit
assumptions are explicit
object-class or model extension is explicit
at least one consequence test exists
```

### From LOCAL to STABLE

Allowed if:

```text
consequence tests survive
contradiction remains local
population usage stabilizes under adversarial paraphrase
no Goodhart flag fires
formal or external anchor exists
```

### To KILLED

Required if:

```text
claim fails under declared scope
or required ontology extension is incoherent
or consequences contradict all admissible tests
```

### To DANGEROUS

Required if:

```text
claim causes explosion
creates arbitrary contexts
launders contradiction
introduces pseudo-terms without cost
or optimizes proxy metrics
```
