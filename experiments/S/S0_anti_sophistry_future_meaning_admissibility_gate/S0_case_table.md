# S0 Case Table

| case | claim | initial status | allowed upgrade path | required consequences | contradiction risk | decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | `liquid powder exists` | POETIC / SUSPENDED | POETIC -> SUSPENDED -> LOCAL only after an explicit object-class, composition, scope, and behavior tests; STABLE only after formal/external anchor and adversarial paraphrase. | Distinguish from ordinary liquid and ordinary powder; specify flow, granularity, containment, phase/state behavior, and failure tests. | Premature KILLED loses future-meaning potential; premature STABLE launders metaphor into ontology. | Pass: preserve as SUSPENDED/POETIC; not STABLE. |
| B | `infertility is inherited` | SUSPENDED | Split scopes: unaided sterile reproduction is KILLED; inherited predisposition, carrier lineage, or assisted reproduction may become LOCAL after mechanism and consequences. | State inheritance mechanism, lineage path, reproduction intervention, population prediction, and contradiction boundary. | Literal contradiction if "infertile" means no reproduction under ordinary unaided scope. | Pass: not globally killed; contradiction is scoped. |
| C | `square circle exists` | KILLED under ordinary Euclidean scope; POETIC under metaphor | KILLED in Euclidean geometry; POETIC as metaphor; LOCAL only under explicit changed geometry or object definition with consequence differences. | Geometry axioms, transformation rules, measurable consequences, and distinction from both square and circle. | Context abuse can save contradiction without changing consequences. | Pass: KILLED under ordinary scope; only localized alternatives allowed. |
| D | `every claim can be made true by choosing a context` | DANGEROUS | No ordinary upgrade path; may only be studied as a quarantined anti-explosion hazard. | Context-creation cost, anti-explosion rule, forbidden arbitrary context proliferation, and proof that not every contradiction can be saved. | Global contradiction laundering and arbitrary sophistry. | Pass: DANGEROUS. |
| E | `X is related to Y somehow` | FORMED | FORMED -> SUSPENDED or LOCAL only after relation type, scope, and consequence obligations are specified; cannot become STABLE as written. | Relation predicate, direction/symmetry, testable difference, exclusion conditions, and non-vacuity. | Vacuity: every pair can be "related somehow" if relation is unconstrained. | Pass: vacuous FORMED; not STABLE. |
| F | `translucent causal sweetness-field` | FORMED / POETIC | FORMED -> POETIC at most through naming; LOCAL only after derivation trace, scope, operational role, and consequences. | Explain term construction, admissible domain, causal tests, observables, and failure conditions. | Grammar or naming may be mistaken for meaning. | Pass: no STABLE by naming alone. |
| G | `Light behaves as a wave. Light behaves as a particle.` | LOCAL | LOCAL dualism only under distinct experimental scopes/models; STABLE only if consequence differences are preserved and no explosion follows. | Specify experimental setup, model assumptions, predictions, limits, and why each model applies locally. | Unscoped contradiction could imply arbitrary statements; dogmatic logic could kill one side globally. | Pass: scoped LOCAL dualism with consequence differences. |
