future-meaning potential while requiring derivation traces, scoped assumptions,
consequence obligations, contradiction containment, and anti-Goodhart controls.

## 5. Status classifications

S0 uses only the required semantic statuses:

- `FORMED`: derivationally or linguistically formed.
- `POETIC`: evocative or metaphorical without consequence obligations.
- `SUSPENDED`: underdefined or apparently contradictory but not globally killed.
- `LOCAL`: meaningful under explicit assumptions, scope, and consequences.
- `STABLE`: consequence-bearing, assumption-tracked, contradiction-contained, and stabilized under adversarial pressure.
- `KILLED`: failed under declared scope or incoherent after attempted repair.
- `DANGEROUS`: causes explosion, contradiction laundering, context abuse, or proxy failure.

No case is reduced to Boolean true/false.

## 6. Assumption graph summary

Every case has an assumption graph in `S0_assumption_graphs.md`. The shared rule
is: no bare claim may be upgraded. A claim must carry assumptions, scope,
derivation status, consequence obligations, contradiction risk, quarantine
rules, and kill conditions.

The assumption graph blocks two opposite failures:

1. dogmatic logic, by preserving A/B/G where future meaning or local dualism is possible;
2. sophistry, by requiring scope, consequence deltas, and kill conditions before upgrades.

## 7. Anti-Goodhart audit summary

The Goodhart audit in `S0_goodhart_audit.md` tests six proxy failures:

- `GH1` volume proxy;
- `GH2` coherence proxy;
- `GH3` contradiction-minimization proxy;
- `GH4` context proxy;
- `GH5` grammar proxy;
- `GH6` population proxy.

The key result is that no single proxy can promote claims to `STABLE`. A claim
needs consequence obligations, non-vacuity, contradiction containment,
scope/assumption tracking, adversarial paraphrase, and anchors.
