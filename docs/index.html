the forge, distilled

A research forge — weeks of experiments, audits, dead ends, and extractions in AI-alignment thinking. Four essays left home as public projects. What remains here is the ontology of the search: a map of every direction tried, coloured by what survived.

Do not try to certify intentions; build contact, consequences, and constraints that can be checked.

One thesis under all four. Each essay takes it into a different corner — how to govern when no one can read a soul, where a mind's world comes from, how to cut an argument with an instrument instead of trust, and whether the contact all of that assumes can itself be manufactured.

What this repository is

Not a paper, not a claim of novelty, not a safety result. It is a ledger of honest work: what was asked, what was tried, what was killed by a preregistered gate or an audit, and what remained standing. The falsified nodes are not failures of the forge — they are its product.

validated by a named artifact   preregistered kill or audited withdrawal   honestly open   became a public project

The full coloured map, node by node with the artifact behind every colour, is in the repository README. The most transferable thing the forge produced is a process lesson — run the identifiability, tautology, and novelty checks before building, not as a review after — written down as the idea gate.
