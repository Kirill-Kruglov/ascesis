# 1. Shift of Research Object

Observation

The project has gradually shifted its focus.

Original engineering question:

> Which environment should be used?

Current research question:

> Under what conditions does a computational agent construct an internal model?

No conclusion follows from this observation.

It merely changes the level of description.

---

# 2. Interaction May Be the Primary Object

Working observation.

Instead of

Agent

and

Environment

consider

Agent × Environment

as the elementary object.

Internal models may emerge from interaction dynamics rather than from either
component alone.

Status

Working hypothesis.

Needs literature review.

---

# 3. Possible Analogy with Evolution

Research intuition.

The interaction-first viewpoint resembles biological reasoning in which
selection acts on phenotype expressed through interaction with the environment,
rather than on isolated genes.

Important

This is an analogy only.

It provides no evidence.

Potential methodological principle:

Cross-domain analogies generate hypotheses.

They never constitute proof.

---

# 4. Interaction Model vs World Model

Open question.

Perhaps successful behaviour requires modelling the structure of interactions
rather than constructing a complete world representation.

Candidate research question:

Can an interaction model be sufficient where a world model appears unnecessary?

No evidence currently favours either position.

---

SD0.A1
