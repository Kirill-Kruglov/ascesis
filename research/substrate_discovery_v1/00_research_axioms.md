# Research Axioms

## Status

Research charter.

This document defines the methodological principles governing the
Substrate Discovery phase.

These are not mathematical axioms.

They are research constraints.

Every future hypothesis, experiment and candidate substrate should remain
consistent with these principles unless there is explicit evidence that one
must be revised.

---

# Why This Document Exists

The Justitia investigation demonstrated an important lesson.

A research programme can gradually optimise intermediate objectives while
remaining internally consistent.

The purpose of these axioms is to preserve the original objective throughout
future exploration.

---

# RA-0 — Goal Anchor

The long-term objective is unchanged.

Construct environments in which a computational learner acquires an internal
world model by interacting with lawful structure rather than by statistically
absorbing human-produced descriptions.

Everything else is subordinate to this objective.

---

# RA-1 — The Research Object

The object of study is not a particular environment.

The object of study is the emergence of internal world models in interacting
computational agents.

Environments are instruments.

They are not the research objective.

---

# RA-2 — Substrates Are Means

No substrate is privileged.

Justitia was one candidate.

Future candidates should be evaluated by the same criteria.

No implementation should become part of the Goal Anchor.

---

# RA-3 — Negative Knowledge Is Knowledge

Every rigorous falsification permanently reduces the search space.

Negative results should be preserved with the same care as successful ones.

The search procedure should accumulate exclusions rather than preferences.

---

# RA-4 — Analytical Before Computational

Whenever possible,

questions should be attacked analytically before expensive implementation.

Only hypotheses surviving analytical scrutiny deserve substantial engineering
investment.

---

# RA-5 — Every Definition Has Status

Every introduced concept must explicitly state whether it is:

FACT

OBSERVATION

WORKING DEFINITION

HYPOTHESIS

Unknown concepts must never silently become accepted facts.

---

# RA-6 — Every Hypothesis Requires a Kill-Gate

No hypothesis may enter the programme without a plausible path to
falsification.

If no imaginable observation could reject it,

it is outside the scope of this research.

---

# RA-7 — Simplicity Is Not Evidence

Compactness,

elegance,

or mathematical beauty

must never be interpreted as evidence of correctness.

The history of the Justitia investigation demonstrates this repeatedly.

---

# RA-8 — Faithfulness Before Formalism

Formal properties are valuable only if they preserve the behavioural property
of interest.

A formally elegant abstraction that loses behavioural fidelity is considered
unsuccessful.

---

# RA-9 — Derivation Over Memorisation

Candidate substrates should reward the discovery of lawful structure.

Whenever successful behaviour can be achieved primarily by memorisation,
lookup or proxy modelling,

the substrate is unsuitable for the Door-1 objective.

---

# RA-10 — Proxy Worlds Are Failure Modes

Learning human descriptions of a world is fundamentally different from
learning the lawful structure generating that world.

Every candidate should therefore be evaluated for proxy-world risk.

---

# RA-11 — Search the Space, Not the Favourite

The programme should optimise expected information gain,

not attachment to a particular candidate.

Research effort should preferentially target candidates that are:

- analytically falsifiable;
- computationally inexpensive to test;
- capable of producing durable knowledge whether they succeed or fail.

---

# RA-12 — Preserve Levels of Reasoning

Questions belonging to different levels should not be mixed.

Level 0:
Goal.

Level 1:
Necessary conditions.

Level 2:
Classes of computable environments.

Level 3:
Candidate substrate families.

Level 4:
Concrete implementations.

Level 5:
Experiments.

Arguments valid at one level must not automatically be transferred to another.

---

# RA-13 — Revision Is Expected

The programme is expected to revise:

- candidate substrates;
- working definitions;
- analytical tools;
- mathematical frameworks.

The Goal Anchor is revised only under explicit evidence.

---

# Closing Principle

The purpose of the programme is not to defend hypotheses.

The purpose is to progressively identify the smallest class of environments
capable of making internal world-model construction the rational strategy for
a computational learner.

Everything else is negotiable.
