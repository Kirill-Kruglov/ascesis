# Purpose

This appendix is intended to serve as the canonical index of the current epistemic state of the ASCESIS project.

Unlike the Research Ontology, which explains the surviving knowledge, this ledger is intentionally operational.

Its purpose is to make every important statement independently auditable.

Every statement appearing in the ontology should correspond to one entry in this ledger.

Each entry records:

- current status,
- confidence level,
- supporting evidence,
- remaining alternative explanations,
- possible falsification experiment.

The ledger is intended to evolve after every completed experiment.

No entry should ever be deleted.

Instead, entries are revised while preserving historical traceability.

# Closing Statement

The purpose of this ledger is not to summarize the project.

Its purpose is to ensure that every significant scientific claim made within ASCESIS remains permanently connected to the evidence from which it was derived.

The ledger therefore represents the project's primary mechanism for epistemic traceability.

Future ontology revisions should modify existing entries rather than replacing them, thereby preserving the complete history of the project's evolving understanding.
